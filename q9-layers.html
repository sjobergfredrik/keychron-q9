Keychron Q9 – layers

macOS, input source Swedish – Pro. Small grey letter = printed keycap. Grey key = nothing new on that layer (works as normal).

Good to know

  • Shift works on top of Fn: Shift+Fn+1 = !, Shift+Fn+8 = (, Shift+Fn+Tab = ° (small labels top right).
  • ~ is a dead key: Fn+Ä, then Space.
  • Caps Lock: tap = Esc (fires on release), hold = navigation layer.
  • Select text: hold Caps + S (⇧), then J/L. Add D (⌥) for whole words, F (⌘) to the end of the line.
  • Knob: turn = volume, press = mute. With Fn2 held: turn = key lighting brightness, press = lighting on/off.
